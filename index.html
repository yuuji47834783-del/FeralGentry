Privacy Policy for FeralGentry

Last updated: September 15, 2026

FeralGentry is a small independent shop selling printable wall art on Etsy. This application is a private tool used solely by the shop owner to publish the shop's own pins to the shop owner's own Pinterest account and boards.

Data We Collect

This application does not collect, store, or share any personal data belonging to third parties. It does not have end users other than the shop owner. The only data handled is the shop owner's own Pinterest authentication token, used solely to publish content to the shop owner's own account.

Data We Do Not Do

We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties. We do not use Pinterest data for advertising or profiling. We do not cache or store Pinterest API data beyond what is required to publish a pin.
